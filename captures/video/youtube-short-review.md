The audio was not transcribed because YouTube provides no captions for this video.

Earlier investigation reviewed the then-loaded comments and replies without finding a badge clue; see `captures/findings.md`. The short's predating the badge makes it likely the badge designer selected an existing viral clip rather than planting its visual details, but that is an inference, not proof.
